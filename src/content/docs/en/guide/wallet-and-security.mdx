## Two-Factor Authentication & App Lock

- **Cloud Password (2FA):** Set an additional password with an optional hint and recovery email in **Settings → Privacy & Security**. Even if someone intercepts an email verification code, login cannot complete without your 2FA password.
- **Local App Lock:** Protect the client locally with a PIN code or OS biometrics (Face ID, Touch ID, Windows Hello, Android Biometric).
- **Session management:** Review every active device model, IP address, and last active time, terminate individual devices remotely, and set an automatic inactive session TTL.

## Granular Privacy Controls

Configure who can see your **Profile Photo**, **Bio**, **Last Seen / Online status**, **Birthday**, **Forwarded messages**, and who can **Call** or add you to **Groups & Channels** (`Everybody`, `Contacts`, or `Nobody`), with per-user `Always Share` and `Never Share` exceptions.

<Callout type="info" title="Public web profile links">
  When someone opens `https://theaver.app/u/<username>` in a browser without logging in, Theaver hides your profile photo and bio automatically unless their visibility is set to **Everybody**.
</Callout>

## Walt Wallet (`THV`)

Theaver integrates the **`@walt`** wallet Mini App and ledger for fast ecosystem transfers:

- **Checks:** Create shareable single-use or multi-claim `THV` checks inside chats.
- **Direct transfers:** Send `THV` directly to any `@username`.
- **Bot & Mini App payments:** Pay for digital items inside bots and Mini Apps with instant `successful_payment` settlement.
