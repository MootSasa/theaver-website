## Two-Factor Authentication and App Lock

In **Settings → Privacy & Security**, you can add a cloud 2FA password with an optional hint and recovery email. Even if someone gets hold of an email login code, they still cannot sign in without that second password. You can also lock the app locally with a PIN code or system biometrics (Face ID, Touch ID, Windows Hello, or Android fingerprint).

The active sessions list displays every signed-in device model, IP address, and last active timestamp. You can sign out unfamiliar devices individually or set an automatic expiration timer for idle sessions.

## Privacy rules

You decide who sees your **Profile Photo**, **Bio**, **Last Seen / Online status**, **Birthday**, **Forwarded messages**, and who can **Call** or add you to **Groups & Channels** (`Everybody`, `Contacts`, or `Nobody`). For fine-tuning, each setting accepts `Always Share` and `Never Share` user exceptions.

<Callout type="info" title="Public profile links">
  When someone visits `https://theaver.app/u/<username>` in a browser without signing in, Theaver automatically hides your profile photo and bio unless their visibility is set to **Everybody**.
</Callout>

## Account deletion

You can configure automatic account removal after long inactivity (1 month to 2 years) or delete your account yourself anytime—either in **Settings → Privacy & Security → Delete Account** or via the [web deletion form](/delete-account). Confirming signs out all devices right away and permanently wipes your data after a 7-day grace window. Signing back in within those 7 days cancels deletion automatically.

## Walt wallet (`THV`)

The built-in **`@walt`** wallet tracks internal virtual **`THV`** balances. You can send `THV` to any `@username`, issue single-use or multi-claim checks inside chats, and pay for digital features in bots and Mini Apps. `THV` units work strictly inside the Theaver platform and are not fiat currency or cryptocurrency.
