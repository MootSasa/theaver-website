Theaver runs natively on Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, iOS, and the web. Your conversation history, drafts, unread positions, and media stay synchronized across every signed-in device.

## 1. Choose your client

Grab an installer from [Downloads](/downloads) or launch the web app at [app.theaver.app](https://app.theaver.app):

- **Windows:** standard `.exe` installer and portable `.zip` archive.
- **macOS:** universal `.zip` for Apple Silicon and Intel, plus architecture-specific builds.
- **Linux:** `.tar.gz` archive paired with a detached `.asc` signature.
- **Android & iOS:** architecture-split or universal `.apk` packages and `.ipa` builds.

<Callout type="tip" title="Release channels">
  Three release tracks run side by side: **Stable** (`app.theaver.app`), **Beta** (`beta.theaver.app`), and **Alpha** (`alpha.theaver.app`). Switch channels anytime under **Settings → About App**.
</Callout>

## 2. Sign in or link a device

- **New accounts:** verify your email code (plus Reverse-SMS or OAuth when offered in your region) and claim a unique `@username`.
- **Existing accounts:** sign in using your username or email + password (and cloud 2FA password if enabled), or connect directly through supported OAuth providers.
- **QR linking:** on an already signed-in phone, open **Settings → Devices** and scan the QR code shown on your desktop or browser screen.

When you connect from a third-party client built with a custom `api_id`, **Settings → Devices** clearly displays the custom app's name, platform icon, and verified publisher badge so you always know which client holds access.

## 3. Shareable `theaver.app` links

Profiles, bots, public channels, and invites resolve cleanly through short links on the main domain:

- Profile or bot: `https://theaver.app/u/<username>`
- Public channel or group: `https://theaver.app/c/<slug>`
- Invite link: `https://theaver.app/join/<code>`

Opening any of these links in a browser renders a verified preview card with a quick button to hand off to the native desktop or mobile app (`theaver://`) or continue straight in the browser.
