Theaver runs across Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, iOS, and modern browsers. All apps connect to the same account and synchronize chats, read markers, drafts, and media automatically.

## 1. Choose your client

Download a verified build from [Downloads](/downloads) or launch the browser client directly at [app.theaver.app](https://app.theaver.app):

- **Windows (x64):** Setup installer (`.exe`) or portable `.zip` archive.
- **macOS (Apple Silicon / Intel):** Universal `.zip` bundle as well as architecture-specific slices.
- **Linux (x64):** Standalone `.tar.gz` archive with a detached `.asc` GPG signature.
- **Android:** `arm64-v8a`, `armeabi-v7a`, `x86_64`, or Universal `.apk`.
- **iOS:** Sideloadable `.ipa` package.

<Callout type="tip" title="Release channels">
  Theaver publishes updates on three channels: **Stable** (`app.theaver.app`), **Beta** (`beta.theaver.app`), and **Alpha** (`alpha.theaver.app`). You can switch your native update channel inside **Settings → About Theaver**.
</Callout>

## 2. Sign in and link devices

1. **New accounts:** Register with an email address, verify the one-time code, and choose a unique `@username`.
2. **Existing accounts:** Sign in with your username/email and password (plus your Two-Factor Authentication password if enabled).
3. **QR Login on Desktop & Web:** Open **Settings → Devices** on an already signed-in mobile phone and scan the QR code displayed by your desktop or browser session.

## 3. Sharing profile and channel links

Every public profile, bot, channel, and invite uses short links on `theaver.app`:

- Profile or bot link: `https://theaver.app/u/<username>`
- Public channel or group: `https://theaver.app/c/<slug>`
- Chat or community invite: `https://theaver.app/join/<code>`

Opening any of these links in a browser renders a preview card with one-click buttons to launch the native client (`theaver://`) or open the thread on `app.theaver.app`.
