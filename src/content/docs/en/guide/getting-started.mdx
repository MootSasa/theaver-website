Theaver runs on Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, iOS, and right in your browser. Your chats, drafts, read markers, and media stay tied to your account and show up across every device you sign in on.

## 1. Pick your app

Grab an installer from [Downloads](/downloads) or open the web client at [app.theaver.app](https://app.theaver.app):

- **Windows:** standard `.exe` installer or portable `.zip` archive.
- **macOS:** universal `.zip` bundle as well as Apple Silicon and Intel builds.
- **Linux:** standalone `.tar.gz` archive paired with a `.asc` GPG signature.
- **Android & iOS:** `.apk` packages (per-ABI or universal) and `.ipa` builds.

<Callout type="tip" title="Release channels">
  Updates ship on three channels: **Stable** (`app.theaver.app`), **Beta** (`beta.theaver.app`), and **Alpha** (`alpha.theaver.app`). Switch channels anytime in **Settings → About Theaver**.
</Callout>

## 2. Sign in and link devices

To create a new account, enter your email, confirm it with the one-time code, and pick a unique `@username`. Existing users sign in with their username or email and password (plus their cloud 2FA password if enabled).

On desktop or in a browser, you can skip typing your password altogether: open **Settings → Devices** on a phone that is already signed in and scan the QR code on screen.

## 3. Short links on `theaver.app`

Public profiles, bots, channels, and group invites use clean short links:

- Profile or bot: `https://theaver.app/u/<username>`
- Public channel or group: `https://theaver.app/c/<slug>`
- Invite link: `https://theaver.app/join/<code>`

Opening any of them in a browser shows a preview card with buttons to jump into the native app (`theaver://`) or continue on `app.theaver.app`.
