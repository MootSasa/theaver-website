Theaver supports direct messages, groups with forum topics, broadcast channels with comments, Discord-style communities, and real-time voice/video calls.

## Direct chats, replies & formatting

- **Rich text & entities:** Supports bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, inline code, preformatted blocks, spoilers, and custom inline emojis.
- **Cloud drafts:** Draft messages and reply targets synchronize automatically across your devices.
- **Message reactions & pins:** Add up to 3 distinct emoji reactions per message and pin important announcements per chat.
- **High-Water Mark read sync:** Unread counters and read positions synchronize atomically across every active session.

## Groups, channels & communities

- **Public channels (`/c/<slug>`):** Broadcast posts with view counters, linked discussion groups, comments, and scheduled messages.
- **Forum topics:** Split large groups into organized topic threads while keeping unread badges accurate per topic.
- **Polls & quizzes:** Create single-choice, multiple-choice, anonymous, or quiz polls with explanations.
- **Communities:** Combine text, voice, and stage channels under categories with role-based permissions, soundboards, and invite links.

## Voice, video & screen sharing

1:1 calls and group voice rooms run on **LiveKit SFU** with **Coturn** over TLS port 443 (`turn.theaver.app`), ensuring calls connect reliably behind restrictive NATs and firewalls.

- **Screen sharing:** Share individual windows or full displays at up to `1080p60`.
- **Voice & round video notes:** Recorded in Opus / MP4 with synchronized `listened` state.
- **OBS / external streams:** Communities support RTMP and WHIP ingress streams alongside server-side recording.
