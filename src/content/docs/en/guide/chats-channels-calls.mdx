Theaver brings together direct chats, groups with forum topics, broadcast channels with comments, role-based communities, and voice rooms.

## Direct chats and formatting

Messages support bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, inline code, preformatted code blocks, spoilers, and custom animated emoji inline inside sentences.

Unfinished messages and selected replies sync as cloud drafts across your signed-in devices. You can leave up to three reactions per message, pin important updates to the top of a chat, and count on unread badges staying accurate across phone, desktop, and web.

## Groups, channels, and communities

Public channels (`/c/<slug>`) include post view counts, scheduled posts, and an optional linked group for reader comments.

Large groups can split conversations into separate forum topics, each tracking its own unread count. Chats also support single-choice, multi-choice, anonymous, and quiz polls with explanations. Communities add structured categories of text, voice, and stage channels with role permissions, soundboards, and invite links.

## Voice, video notes, and calls

One-on-one calls and group voice rooms run over encrypted media channels designed to connect reliably even behind strict office or campus firewalls.

During calls, you can share a single application window or an entire display at up to `1080p` and `60 fps`. Voice notes and round video messages record in one tap and track when the other person listens. For community live streams, you can feed OBS Studio directly via RTMP or WHIP.
