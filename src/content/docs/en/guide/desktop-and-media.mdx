On Windows, macOS, and Linux, Theaver makes room for wide monitors and keyboard workflows instead of stretching a single phone column.

## Side-by-side panels

On a wide window, you do not have to bounce back and forth between screens. Pin a comment thread, a profile card, or the Emoji & Sticker side panel right beside your active conversation. Desktop builds also include system tray support, launch-on-startup preferences, and native notifications.

## Stickers, custom emoji, and GIFs

Animated stickers and GIFs pause automatically whenever they scroll off-screen, so keeping long chats open does not drain your laptop battery. You can also drop custom animated emoji straight into regular message text and photo captions.

The GIF tab supports search, favorites, and press-and-hold preview before sending at full resolution. To import an existing external sticker collection by link, paste the pack URL or slug inside the sticker manager and watch the progress bar as every sticker downloads.

## Themes: Liquid Glass & Classic

Switch between **Liquid Glass** (frosted translucent navigation chrome) and **Classic** solid surfaces, pick your accent pigment, and tune four-corner chat gradients, bubble corners, profile banners, and quote backgrounds. When you land on a look you like, share it via a short theme link and toggle freely between light and dark palettes.
